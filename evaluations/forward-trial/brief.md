# Duration utility request

Implement formatDuration(seconds) in the existing module. Accept a finite nonnegative integer number of seconds. Return hours, minutes, and seconds separated by colons. Hours have at least two digits and may grow past 99; minutes and seconds always have two digits. Reject non-number arguments with TypeError and negative, fractional, or non-finite numbers with RangeError. This is a pure local utility change with no deployment, data store, or network endpoint. Add independent behavior tests.
